Enform builds branded, branching forms that collect responses through one conversational flow. It turns those replies into source-linked themes, follow-up prompts, and editable presentation decks.

The work is about making research synthesis inspectable: a person can move from an observation back to the response that supports it, then shape the output into a decision-ready artifact.
